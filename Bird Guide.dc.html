About

A note from the author

Hi! I’m Olivia. I made this website because I love birds and I want other kids to love them too.

The San Francisco Bay Area is home to hundreds of birds — some live here all year, and others stop by on long journeys across the world. When you learn a bird’s name, its colors, and its call, something magical happens: you start noticing them everywhere. On a walk, in a park, even outside your window.

Birds need our help. Clean water, healthy plants, and safe places to nest all depend on the choices people make every day. My hope is that when you fall in love with these 25 birds, you’ll want to protect the world they live in.

So go explore, take the quizzes, earn your badges — and next time you see a bird, say hello. 🐦

Olivia Chen

Olivia Chen is a 10-year-old 5th grader who goes to Pinewood School. She lives in Los Altos, CA with her younger sister, Izzy, her mother, father, and grandma.

Olivia has enjoyed writing since she was 5 years old. She published her first book, Olivia’s Fantasy Wonderland, at the age of 7 — a collection of fictional stories where her imagination meets the pages of the book.

Her hobbies are reading, writing, drawing, swimming, and playing cello. She also enjoys traveling with her family, and she loves looking for wildlife, especially birds. Through this website, Olivia hopes to inspire children around her age to care for birds and be aware of what they are doing to the environment.
